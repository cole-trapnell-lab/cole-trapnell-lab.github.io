I am a Genome Sciences graduate student in the Trapnell Lab. Prior to enrolling in 
graduate school, I worked in the Department of Orthopedic Surgery and Sports 
Medicine at the University of Washington studying the genetic risk factors of skeletal 
diseases. At the Trapnell lab, I want to enhance my understanding of the genetic 
mechanisms that drive developmental processes and their potential links to disease.

loved ones!
